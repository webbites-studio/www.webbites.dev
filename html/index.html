Questions

Frequently asked

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), the text about your business and services, and any photos you'd like to use. Links to a few websites you like help us match your style.

What is a design variant?

A distinct design concept for your site. You review the variants, pick your favorite, and we build the full site in that style.

What counts as a change request?

One round of feedback you send after reviewing your site, such as a list of text edits, color tweaks, or layout adjustments. Plans include 3, 5, or 8 rounds.

How long does it take?

The Fast plan ($333) is delivered in 14 days. Popular and Best plans include more pages and design work, so we agree on a firm delivery date with you before you order.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The code belongs to you. You can manage the site yourself, keep it on WebBites hosting, or take it to another developer whenever you like.

What happens if I stop hosting with you?

You can cancel anytime. We hand over your site files so you can move them to any host or developer.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You pay the plan price, $15/month hosting after the free period, and optional support ($50/hour) or urgent updates ($100). Your domain name is registered in your own name and paid directly to the registrar.
