How Much Does a Small Business Website Cost in 2026?

Every small business owner asks this question — and gets a frustrating answer: “it depends.” Here's the honest version. In 2026, a small business website costs anywhere from $100 to $15,000+, depending on which route you take. The price you pay should depend on what you need — not on how much mystery a designer wraps around their pricing.

The four routes, with real price ranges

Route 1

Do it yourself

$100–$500/year

Website builders like Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress.com cost little in cash and a lot in time. If you enjoy tinkering and your site is a simple one-pager, this works. The trade-off: your evenings disappear into templates, and “good enough” design often costs you customers who judge you in the first five seconds.

Route 2

Hire a freelancer

$500–$5,000

A good freelancer can build you a solid site. The risk is consistency: pricing varies wildly, timelines slip, and once the project ends, you're often on your own for updates.

Route 3

Hire an agency

$3,000–$15,000+

Agencies deliver polished work with a team behind it. They also charge for the team. For a local bakery, salon, or contractor, agency pricing is usually more site than the business needs.

Route 4

Flat-fee package

From $333

A newer model: fixed packages with everything included — design, pages, mobile optimization — at a published flat price. No quotes, no hourly billing, no “it depends.”

What your money actually buys

Whatever route you take, make sure the price includes:

  • ✓ A modern design that works on phones (most of your visitors are on mobile)
  • ✓ The pages you actually need — home, services, about, contact — not filler
  • ✓ Basic search setup so Google can find you
  • ✓ A clear answer to: what happens after launch? Hosting, updates, and support are recurring costs — typically $10–$50/month for hosting alone.

Watch out for these

Designers who won't publish prices

If the price is a secret, the price is usually high.

“Free website” offers

They can lock you into expensive monthly plans or keep your domain hostage.

Quotes that don't include hosting and updates

A $500 site that needs $100/month in hidden fees isn't a $500 site.

Timelines with no end date

Ask when your site goes live. We put ours in writing: 14 days on our $333 package.
