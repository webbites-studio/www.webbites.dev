Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information WebBites ("we", "us") collects when you visit webbites.dev or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit the order form, we receive your name, email address or phone number, the plan and project type you choose, and your message. We use this only to reply to your request, prepare a quote or proposal, and deliver the services you order.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. We use Google Tag Manager, which may load Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, device type, approximate location). These tools may set cookies. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Spam protection. Our forms and contact details are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which processes technical signals from your browser to tell humans from bots. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Preferences. Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site and handle your request (such as Cloudflare and Google), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details as long as needed to respond to you and provide our services, and then as long as required for business or legal records.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Residents of some US states, such as California, have additional rights under state privacy laws. To make a request, use the contact form or the contact details in the site footer.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
